About

Paste two versions of a text, or open two files, and the differences appear straight away. Removed lines are shown in red and added lines in green, with the exact words or characters that changed highlighted inside each line. Switch between a side-by-side view and a single unified view, hide long stretches of unchanged lines, and ignore changes in spacing or letter case. You can copy or download the result as a standard .diff patch. It works with plain text, code, CSV, JSON, HTML and other text files. Everything runs in your browser, so your text never leaves your device.
