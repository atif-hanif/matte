    About

    A meta description is the short summary Google may show under your page title in search results. A good one includes the main keyword, says clearly what the page offers and ends with a reason to click. Fill in your keyword, brand, location and main benefits, choose the page type and tone, and get ten different descriptions to pick from. Each one shows its length in characters and in pixels, because Google cuts descriptions off by width, usually around 920 pixels or roughly 155–160 characters on desktop. The Google preview shows how the result may look, with your keyword in bold. You can edit any description and its length updates as you type. Google sometimes rewrites descriptions using text from the page, so make sure your page content matches what the description promises. Everything runs in your browser.
