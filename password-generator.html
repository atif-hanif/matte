    About

    Generate random passwords from uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers and symbols, from 4 to 128 characters long, or create passphrases made of easy-to-type words. Every password contains at least one character from each type you select, and you can leave out look-alike characters such as l, 1, I, O and 0. The strength meter shows how many bits of randomness each password has and roughly how long it would take to guess. Passwords are made with your browser's secure random number generator (Web Crypto), so nothing is sent to a server and nothing is saved.
