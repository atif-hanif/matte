    About

    The title tag is the blue headline Google shows for your page in search results, and it is one of the strongest signals of what a page is about. A good title starts with the main keyword, says what makes the page worth clicking and ends with your brand. Fill in your keyword, brand, location, audience and main benefit, choose the page type and style, and get ten titles to pick from. Each title shows its length in characters and in pixels, because Google cuts titles off by width, usually around 600 pixels or roughly 50–60 characters. The Google preview shows how the result may look. You can edit any title and its length updates as you type. Google sometimes rewrites titles that are too long, vague or stuffed with keywords, so keep yours clear and true to the page. Everything runs in your browser.
