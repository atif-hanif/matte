About

Paste XML or open a file (.xml, .svg, .rss, .xsd, .config, .plist and more) and get it back minified onto a single line. Whitespace between tags is removed, comments can be stripped, and empty elements can be shortened to . CDATA sections and elements marked xml:space="preserve" are left exactly as written. The tool also checks that every tag is closed and tells you the line of the first problem. Everything runs in your browser, so your files never leave your device.
