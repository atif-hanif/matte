Check a username

Type a username to see which sites accept its length and characters. This checks each site's naming rules only. It does not check whether the name is already taken.

About

Generate usernames for Instagram, TikTok, X, YouTube, Discord, games and any other account. Pick a style such as Cool, Gamer, Professional, Cute or Short, or type your name and a few words you like to get usernames built around them. You can choose a separator, upper or lower case, a maximum length, numbers and l33t letters. Star the ones you like to keep them in the Saved list, then copy them or download them as a .txt file. The checker shows which sites allow a username's length and characters. Site rules can change, and none of the names are checked for availability, so search for your favourite on each site before you decide. Everything runs in your browser.
