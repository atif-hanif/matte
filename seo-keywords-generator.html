About

Type the main keyword for a page, product or service, or enter a website link to find the phrases that page already uses, and get hundreds of related search phrases people commonly use, such as questions, comparisons, "best" and "near me" searches, prices, guides and ideas. Add a location to get local keywords, and add extra words such as your industry or platform to get more specific long-tail keywords. Each keyword is tagged by search intent: informational (people want to learn), commercial (they are comparing options), transactional (they are ready to buy or hire) and local. Use informational keywords for blog posts and guides, and commercial and transactional keywords for service and product pages. Click a keyword to select it, then copy your picks or download them all as a CSV file. Some websites block other sites from reading them. If a link does not work, paste the page’s source or text instead. The ideas are built from common search patterns in your browser, so they do not include search volumes. Check the volume and competition of your favourites in a tool such as Google Keyword Planner or Google Search Console before you choose.
