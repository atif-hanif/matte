Next steps
  1. Upload sitemap.xml to the root of your website, so it opens at https://example.com/sitemap.xml.
  2. Add this line to your robots.txt: Sitemap: https://example.com/sitemap.xml
  3. Submit it in Google Search Console under Sitemaps, and in Bing Webmaster Tools.

About

A sitemap is an XML file that lists the pages on your website so Google, Bing and other search engines can find and index them. Enter your website link and the tool follows the links on each page, staying on your domain, until it has found every page it can reach or hits the page limit you set. It skips images, PDFs and other files, links to other websites, and pages marked noindex, and it uses each page's canonical link when it has one. Pages closer to the homepage get a higher priority. You can also paste your own list of links instead of crawling. Download the sitemap.xml, upload it to your website's main folder and submit it in Google Search Console. Some websites block other sites from reading their pages; in that case, the tool reads them through a public proxy, and if that fails too, paste your page links instead. Pages that are only linked from scripts or forms may not be found, so check the list before you upload it.
