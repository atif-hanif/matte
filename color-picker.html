About

Pull the colors out of any picture. Drop in one or more images and see their main colors together in one table, each with its share of the image, or click an image to see only its colors. Use the eyedropper to pick an exact color from any spot. Every color is shown as HEX, RGB, RGBA, HSL, HSLA, HWB and CMYK; click a value to copy it, or download the whole palette as a CSV file. Everything runs in your browser, so your images never leave your device.
