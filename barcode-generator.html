About

Make barcodes for products, shelves, stock labels, shipping cartons and libraries. Choose EAN-13, UPC-A, EAN-8 or UPC-E for retail products, Code 128, Code 39 or Code 93 for labels that need letters, ITF-14 for cartons, or Codabar, MSI and Pharmacode for special uses. For EAN and UPC you can type the number without its last digit and the check digit is added for you. If you type the full number, the tool tells you whether the check digit is right. Change the bar width, height, text and colours, then download a sharp PNG, an SVG for print, or switch to Batch to make up to 500 barcodes at once and download them as a ZIP file. To sell products in shops you need real EAN or UPC numbers registered with GS1 for your company. This tool draws the barcode but does not issue numbers. Everything runs in your browser.
