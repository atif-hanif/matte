About

Convert your JPG photos to WebP and make them lighter for the web. Drop in as many images as you like, choose the quality that suits you, and download each result individually or all together as a ZIP. WebP files are usually much smaller than JPGs with very little visible difference, so pages load faster. Everything runs in your browser, so your images never leave your device.
